Privacy Policy - SyncTune AI

Last updated: October 2026 • Application: SyncTune AI (com.aivideobgm.app)

Welcome to SyncTune AI ("we", "our", or "us"). SyncTune AI is designed to help creators automatically analyze video dynamics and match, synchronize, and export perfectly timed background music. Your privacy is paramount, and we are committed to protecting your personal data and media files.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile photo when signing up via email or Google OAuth.
  • Customer Support: Email address and communication details when you reach out to our team.

1.2 Media and Video Files

  • User-Selected Videos: Videos selected from your device gallery are accessed strictly to analyze tempo, motion dynamics, and scene cut transitions for background music synchronization.
  • Privacy Protection: Processing occurs on-device whenever possible. We do not use your personal video content to train public AI models.

1.3 Device & Storage Permissions

  • Photos / Media Library (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used to allow you to select videos for background music matching.
  • Storage / Media Write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to save your rendered, synchronized videos back to your device photo gallery.
  • Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used to sample, synchronize, and preview audio playback.

1.4 Diagnostics & Usage Data

  • Device model, operating system version, app logs, and crash diagnostics used solely to maintain stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze video pacing and recommend mood-matched royalty-free background music.
  • To perform dynamic audio variations, segment-level cuts, and beat synchronization.
  • To save and synchronize project history across your authenticated sessions.
  • To troubleshoot bugs and respond to customer service inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

We only share data with essential third-party services that enable core app features:

  • Google Identity / OAuth: Secure authentication and sign-in.
  • Supabase: Cloud database for account credentials and saved project metadata.
  • Royalty-Free Audio Providers: Audio tracks licensed under CC BY 4.0 (Incompetech) and CC0 (Pixabay). No personal user data is sent to audio providers.

4. Data Security

Encryption: All data transmitted between the mobile application, web portal, and backend servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS). We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

  • You may view, edit, or request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time.
  • You can request deletion inside the app via Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • You can also submit an automated web deletion request via our portal: SyncTune AI Delete Account Page.
  • All associated account credentials, project history, and uploaded references are permanently purged within 7 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy

SyncTune AI is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
